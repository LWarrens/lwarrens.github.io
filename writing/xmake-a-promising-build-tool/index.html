xmake: a promising build tool

A short note from maintaining an xmake build for a node-addon-api project, and returning to CMake for now.

May 7, 2025
xmakecmakebuild-systems

Playing around with node-addon-api and maintaining my own xmake build was fun.

It is easier to work with than CMake, when you know what you’re doing.

For now, though, I think I’m going to revert to CMake while the project matures.
